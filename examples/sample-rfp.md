**Solicitation No.** DEMO-26-R-0001
**Agency.** Notional Defense Systems Activity (NDSA)
**Title.** Ground Terminal Sustainment Services
**Response due.** 30 calendar days after release
**Contract type.** Firm-fixed-price IDIQ, 5-year ordering period

## Section L — Instructions to Offerors

**L.1 General.** Offerors shall submit two volumes. Volume I (Technical) shall not
exceed 25 pages. Volume II (Price) has no page limit. Submissions shall be in
12-point Times New Roman with one-inch margins, delivered in PDF format via the
agency portal no later than 4:00 PM Eastern on the closing date.

**L.2 Volume I organization.** The Technical Volume shall be organized into the
following sections, in this order:

- L.2.1 Technical Approach
- L.2.2 Management Approach
- L.2.3 Past Performance
- L.2.4 Transition Plan

**L.3 Technical Approach.** The offeror shall describe its approach to sustaining
ground terminal hardware across three geographically separated sites. The offeror
shall describe how it will maintain an operational availability of not less than
95% measured monthly. The offeror shall identify the diagnostic tooling it will
use and shall describe how spare parts will be sourced and staged.

**L.4 Management Approach.** The offeror shall describe its staffing model,
including the labor categories proposed and the basis for the proposed staffing
levels. The offeror shall describe its approach to quality management and shall
identify the quality standard to which it will operate.

**L.5 Past Performance.** The offeror shall submit not more than three past
performance references from the last five years for work of similar scope,
magnitude, and complexity. Each reference shall include the contract number, the
customer point of contact, the period of performance, and the dollar value.

**L.6 Transition Plan.** The offeror shall describe its plan to assume performance
within 45 calendar days of award without degradation of service.

## Section M — Evaluation Factors for Award

Award will be made to the offeror whose proposal represents the best value to the
Government, with technical factors significantly more important than price.

**M.1 Technical Approach (most important).** The Government will evaluate the
extent to which the proposed approach demonstrates a credible method for achieving
and sustaining the required operational availability, and the extent to which the
proposed diagnostic and supply approach reduces mean time to repair.

**M.2 Management Approach (second most important).** The Government will evaluate
the adequacy of the proposed staffing model and the extent to which the offeror's
quality management approach provides visibility into performance.

**M.3 Past Performance (third most important).** The Government will evaluate the
relevance and quality of the offeror's recent past performance.

**M.4 Risk.** The Government will assess the risk associated with the offeror's
proposed approach, including transition risk. Risk will be evaluated as an
element of each technical factor rather than as a separate factor.

C.3.1 The contractor shall perform corrective and preventive maintenance on all
