## 2.1 Technical Approach

Our team sustains operational availability above the 95% monthly threshold at
all three sites. Our sustainment approach covers corrective and preventive
maintenance, spares provisioning, and monthly performance reporting, developed
across eleven years of ground terminal sustainment.

Our approach applies a preventive maintenance methodology that held availability
at 96.4% or better on every month of the prior contract. Our planning team
schedules preventive actions on a 30-day cycle; certified technicians perform
them. Our diagnostic toolset isolates line-replaceable-unit faults in under 40
minutes, and our internal engineering group designed and fielded it across four
programs.

We reduce mean time to repair through structured diagnostic procedures that
narrowed MTTR from 6.2 to 3.8 hours on Contract N00024-22-C-1234. We source
spare parts from qualified suppliers and stage them at each site to a 30-day
demand level, replenished when stock falls below a 14-day threshold. Our supply
chain hands off to site operations through a single documented interface, and we
address each requirement in the compliance matrix at Section 5.

## 2.2 Management Approach

category throughout the period of performance, with named backups for each
position.

We operate quality management to AS9110C. Site leads collect performance metrics
and report them to the program manager by the fifth business day of each month.
Issues that breach a defined threshold escalate within one business day, and the
technical authority named in Section 3.2 resolves them.
